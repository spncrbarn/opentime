Install it

Put it on your home screen.

Open Time installs straight from your browser, no app store needed. You get an icon, a full-screen app, and it works offline.

Your device

iPhone & iPad

In Safari

  1. Open opentime.work/app
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add

Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap Share in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

Your device

Android

In Chrome

  1. Open opentime.work/app
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install

Chrome may also offer an Install banner at the bottom of the screen.

Your device

Mac & PC

In Chrome, Edge or Safari

  1. Open opentime.work/app
  2. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install
  3. Safari on Mac: choose File → Add to Dock

Once it's installed, right-click its icon to keep it in your Dock or taskbar.

Signed in on one device? Sign in with the same email on the others and everything syncs.
